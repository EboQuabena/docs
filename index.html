About

I study how rain falls over West Africa, and how it will change.

I am a climate scientist in the Department of Physics at the University of Cape Coast, where I coordinate the Meteorology and Atmospheric Physics Programme. My research covers rainfall regimes and extremes, mesoscale convective systems, the West African Monsoon under climate change, and the regional effects of proposed climate interventions such as stratospheric aerosol injection.

I combine ground and space observations, from rain gauges and satellites to GNSS and lightning sensors, with regional and global climate models. I host a GPS receiver station at UCC, contributed to the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report, and now serve as a Review Editor for the Seventh.
