Francis Nkrumah (PhD)

I am a climate scientist and Senior Lecturer in the Department of Physics, University of Cape Coast (UCC), Ghana, where I coordinate the Meteorology and Atmospheric Physics Programme. I serve as a Review Editor for the Working Group I contribution to the IPCC Seventh Assessment Report (AR7), and was a Contributing Author to the IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (AR6).

My work focuses on the West African water cycle and climate: rainfall regimes and extremes, mesoscale convective systems, West African Monsoon dynamics under climate change, and the regional impacts of climate intervention. I am part of the EW4Energy project on storm and lightning early warning in Ghana, host a GPS receiver station at UCC, and enjoy teaching and mentoring the next generation of African atmospheric scientists.

Research

My research interests encompass hydro-climatology and the West African water cycle, regional climate modelling, mesoscale convective systems, and the dynamics of the West African Monsoon under climate change. I focus on hydro-meteorological extremes such as droughts, dry and wet spells, and high-intensity precipitation, and on combining ground-based and space-based observations (rain gauges, satellites, GNSS, lightning sensors and reanalyses) with climate models. I am also interested in climate intervention, including solar radiation modification and carbon dioxide removal, with a particular emphasis on their regional implications in West Africa.
